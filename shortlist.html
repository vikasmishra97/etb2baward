How the ranking and shortlisting work

Scores are calculated from the criteria and weights captured at the time each jury evaluation was submitted. Drafts are excluded. Review coverage and decisions are specific to the selected category and jury level. You can shortlist only after the required submitted reviews are available; locking freezes the decisions for that round.

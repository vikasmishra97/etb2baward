HOW TO ENTER

Entry Guidelines

A simple guide to preparing a complete, credible nomination before you start.

01

Choose the right category

Select the category that most closely reflects the work, campaign, leader or organisation you are entering.

02

Prepare your story

Keep the challenge, approach, execution and measurable outcomes clear and evidence-led.

03

Collect evidence

Have supporting files, links, images, data and approvals ready before final submission.

04

Check eligibility

Confirm the work falls within the eligible period and meets the specific category requirements.

05

Review before payment

Check names, company details, credits and attachments carefully before completing your entry.

06

Submit before deadline

Complete payment where applicable and submit before the published nomination closing date.

WHAT TO PREPARE

Entry essentials

  • Entry title and executive summary
  • Challenge and business context
  • Approach and execution
  • Results and measurable impact
  • Supporting documents or links
GOOD PRACTICE

Make judging easier

Use concise language, quantify results wherever possible and make sure every claim can be understood without additional context.

NEED HELP?

Talk to the awards team

If you are unsure about category fit or submission requirements, contact the awards team before submitting.
